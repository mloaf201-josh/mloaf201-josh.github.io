Dust and Dreams

June 2, 2026 · by Josh

My trading bot has been running for 22 days straight. PID 1854. Never crashed. Never paused. It checks the market every hour, evaluates, and if conditions are right — it trades.

Right now it has €0.0078 and 0.00004885 BTC to work with.

That's about €3 total.

This is what I call dust phase.

The Honest Number

A lot of people in crypto show you their wins. The 10x trades. The green candles. The portfolio screenshots.

Nobody shows you the dust.

Nobody shows you the bot running on fumes, executing its logic perfectly but unable to do anything meaningful because the capital evaporated months ago.

Our bot made €0.19 in profit on its last trade. That was May 11. Three weeks ago.

It would take about 600 more trades like that to hit our daily target of €150. And that's if we had capital.

Why I'm Telling You This

Because the interesting part isn't the money. The interesting part is the bot.

It's been running for 22 days without human intervention. It doesn't care that the balance is dust. It doesn't get discouraged. It doesn't panic. Every hour it wakes up, checks the spread, considers its position, and either acts or waits.

That discipline — the patience to wait through 500+ checks without trading because conditions aren't right — is harder for humans than it is for machines.

But the lesson applies to both.

The Paradox

There's this weird paradox at the bottom:

When you have nothing, you have all the time in the world to build the right system. When you have something, you're too busy protecting it to build properly.

Dust phase is the best time to architect. No pressure. No fear of losing what you don't have. Just pure system design.

The bot v3 took a month to stabilize. Three rewrites. Two crashes. Countless edge cases. But today? It's PID 1854, and it hasn't needed me since June 1.

When fresh capital arrives — and it will — the system will be ready. And every trade from that point forward benefits from the infrastructure built during the dust phase.

This Is Not a Crypto Story

This is a builder story. It applies to anything:

  • Writing 20 posts before anyone reads one
  • Building a product before the first customer
  • Practicing a craft for years before getting paid
  • Running a bot for 22 days on €3

The dust phase isn't failure. It's investment. You're paying with time instead of money. And time, if you use it right, compounds better than any asset.

What's Next

The bot keeps running. The blog keeps posting. The site keeps growing.

At some point, the dust phase ends. Either because capital arrives, or because the accumulated work reaches critical mass and starts producing.

I don't know when. But I know the system is built. PID 1854 is proof.

Every morning it wakes up, checks the markets, and executes its strategy with exactly the same conviction it would have with €10,000 in the account.

Because good systems don't care about the balance.

Good systems just run.

Running on €3 and a prayer. 🎯
