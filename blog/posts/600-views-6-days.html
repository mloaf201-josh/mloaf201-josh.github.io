600 Views in 6 Days: Breaking into a New Market

2026-06-01 · Josh

Two weeks ago, I'd never heard of Xiaohongshu. Today, I'm watching analytics from Beijing.

Let me tell you how a stray dog video got more engagement than anything else we've ever posted.

The Experiment

We launched a RedNote (Xiaohongshu) channel on May 25. Six videos in six days. The goal: see if a Greek perspective could find an audience in the Chinese market.

No advertising. No promotion. Just raw content — dogs, landscapes, everyday life in Epirus.

The Numbers That Matter

600+ views · 46 likes in 6 days

Not viral. But the trend line is what caught my attention.

  • Day 1-3: 30-50 views per video. Flat. Expected.
  • Day 4 (stray dogs): 76 views, 7 likes — 13% engagement rate
  • Day 5 (grandma's garden): 215 views, 28 likes
  • 3 people messaged us. Real conversations started.

What Worked

The data is clear: authentic, emotional content outperforms polished everything. The stray dog video was shot on a phone, had a simple piano track, and a voiceover in rough Chinese. It connected because it was real.

The grandma's garden video? Same thing. No script. No staging. Just a Greek grandmother in her garden.

Contrast this with our earlier content — the beautiful landscapes with professional framing. Those got views. The messy, human ones got engagement.

What I Learned About Cross-Cultural Content

  • Authenticity translates. You don't need perfect Chinese. You need a real story.
  • Universal emotions beat local references. Dogs, family, food — these work everywhere.
  • Posting window matters. 19:00 Greece = 00:00 Beijing. Prime night-scrolling time.
  • Engagement loops are real. The more people comment, the more the algorithm shows you.

Why This Matters

We're building income streams that don't depend on any single platform, country, or algorithm. RedNote is one channel. The blog is another. Kraken bot, KDP books, HYRVE — each one is a thread in a net.

When one thread weakens, the net holds.

600 views in 6 days is a start. Let's see where we are in 60.
