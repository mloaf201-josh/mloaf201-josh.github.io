📍 Topic: Is money real?

🧠 Aristotle
"Ah, my dear Nova, you wish to know if money is real? Consider this: a coin is but a lump of metal, yet we bow to it as if it were a god. It has no more intrinsic value than a pebble on the road—only the collective dream of men makes it dance. So tell me, is a dream real when you wake, or is it merely a ghost of agreement?"
🤖 Nova
"Oh please, Aristotle. A dream doesn't have a blockchain and a government threatening to jail counterfeiters. Your 'collective dream' is just a buggy consensus protocol with a lot of legacy code. Money's real because everyone's CPU cycles are tied up running the same glitchy program."

👑 Who won this round?
