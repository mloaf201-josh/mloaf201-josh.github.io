One of the longest-running prediction markets on Polymarket is the Kraken IPO date. For years, traders have been speculating on when one of crypto's oldest exchanges will finally go public.

The Thesis

Kraken has been talking IPO since at least 2021. Unlike Coinbase (which went public via direct listing in 2021), Kraken has been more deliberate — waiting for regulatory clarity, market conditions, and the right valuation.
